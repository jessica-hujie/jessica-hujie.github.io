About Me

Hello! I am Jie Hu (胡婕), a third-year Ph.D. student at the Center for Data Science, Peking University. I am incredibly fortunate to be advised by Prof. Zaiwen Wen and Prof. Kun Yuan. Previously, I obtained my B.S. in Applied Mathematics from Tongji University in 2023.

My research focuses on efficient architecture and inference for Large Langugae Models, Generative Models and Multimodal Large Language Models. I currently also work on AI for Math.
